# Notifications

Apps notify you with a banner at the top right of the screen (top left when the interface is in
Arabic or Hebrew), just below the menu bar.

- A regular notification goes away by itself after a few seconds.
- The ones that need an answer, like a [Clock](help:clock) **alarm**, stay until you press one of
  their buttons (for example “Stop” or “Snooze”).
- Clicking the banner runs its main action (open what it is about, stop the alarm).
- The **×** closes it without doing anything.

Notifications show with any theme and also while your session runs in the background: if you
switched users, they appear when you come back. [Backups](help:backup) let you know when they
finish or if something failed.
