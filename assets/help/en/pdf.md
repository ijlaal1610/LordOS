# PDF Viewer

To read, search, annotate and fill in PDFs, in the style of Preview.

- **View:** thumbnails or outline, zoom (❖ and the wheel, or pinch), continuous or one page at a
  time, rotate and “dark page” for reading at night.
- **Find** (❖F): highlights everything and jumps to the result (Enter, ⇧Enter, ❖G).
- **Go to a page:** ❖⌥G or click “Page N of M”.
- **Copy text:** drag to select and ❖C.
- **Annotate:** highlight (five colors), notes and freehand drawing. ❖Z undoes.
- **Forms:** filled in place.
- **Save:** always **as a copy** (“name (annotated).pdf”), with ❖S. The original is never touched.
- **Print:** ❖P.

If the PDF has a password, it is asked for when opening. PDFs open in a separate, sandboxed process.
