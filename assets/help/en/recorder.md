# Recorder

Voice memos with the microphone.

- **Record** and **stop**; the waveform shows while recording.
- The list keeps every recording: rename, play or **trim** them.
- **Export** to Opus, MP3 or WAV.

The first time it asks for **microphone** permission. The microphone is chosen in
[Settings → Sound](settings:sound).
