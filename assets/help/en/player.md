# Player

Videos and music.

- **Video:** timeline, volume, subtitles (an `.srt` next to the video or the file's own), full
  screen and **picture in picture**.
- **Music:** library by artist and album with artwork, queue, shuffle and repeat.
- **Media keys** (play, pause, next, previous) work anytime, and what's playing shows in Control
  Center.

Videos and music are decoded in a separate, sandboxed process; hardware acceleration (VA-API) is
used when the computer has it.
