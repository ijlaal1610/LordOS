# Scanner

Scans with network scanners that need no driver (eSCL, also called AirScan; most modern
multifunction printers).

1. Choose the scanner: the ones on your network show up by themselves.
2. **Preview** and, if you want, drag to choose the area.
3. Choose color or gray, the resolution and, if it has one, the document feeder.
4. **Scan** and save as **PDF**, **PNG** or **JPEG** in Documents or Pictures.

Printers are added in [Settings → Printers](settings:printers) (see [Printing](help:printing)).
