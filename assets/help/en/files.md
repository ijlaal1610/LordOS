# Files

The file manager.

## Basics

- **Sidebar:** Recents, Applications, your folders (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Music, Pictures,
  Videos), connected disks, tags and the Trash.
- **Views:** icons (❖1), list (❖2), columns (❖3) and gallery (❖4). You can also group by kind,
  date or size.
- **Tabs** (❖T) and **new windows** (❖N).
- **Quick Preview:** Space shows the image, text, PDF or video without opening it; the arrows move
  to the next one.
- **Get Info** (❖I): size, location, dates, permissions and “Open with”.

## Working with files

- **Copy, cut and paste** (❖C, ❖X, Ctrl+V) and **drag** to another folder, the sidebar or a tab.
  Large copies run in the background, with progress at the bottom.
- **Rename:** Enter. **New folder:** ❖⇧N. **Duplicate:** ❖D.
- **Trash:** ❖⌫ or Delete. From the Trash you restore or delete for good.
- **Archives:** double-clicking a `.zip`, `.tar.gz`, `.7z`… opens it like a folder; right-click
  has “Extract here” and “Compress”.
- **Color tags:** right-click → a tag. They then show in the sidebar.
- **Hidden files:** ❖⇧. shows them.

## Shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| ❖[ · ❖] · ❖↑ | Back · forward · enclosing folder |
| ❖↓ or double-click | Open |
| ❖A | Select all |
| ⇧click · ❖click | Select a range · add to selection |

Apps can only write to your home folder and to the disks you connect.
