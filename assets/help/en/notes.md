# Notes

Quick notes in markdown that can also stay on the desktop as sticky notes.

- **New note:** ❖N. It saves itself while you type; the title is the first line.
- **Read or edit:** ❖E, or double-click while reading.
- **Formatting:** ❖B bold, ❖I italic, ❖K link, ❖1 to ❖3 headings, ❖⇧7 numbered list, ❖⇧8
  bullets, ❖⇧9 checkboxes `- [ ]` (tick them with a click) and ❖⇧C code block.
- **Images and attachments:** paste an image (from any app) or pick a file; it is copied into the
  note.
- **On the desktop:** each note can stay as a sticky note, in a color, and be pinned above windows.
- **Delete:** the note goes to the Trash and can be restored.

Notes are regular folders in `~/Notas`, with a `nota.md` inside. Spotlight finds them by title and
by text.
