# Emoji & Symbols

- **❖.** (or Ctrl+❖Space) opens the **quick picker** below the cursor, in any app: search by name
  in your language (“heart”), choose the skin tone and Enter types it.
- The **Emoji & Symbols** app shows every emoji by category and Unicode symbols by block (arrows,
  math, currency…). A click copies or types the character.

Frequently used emoji come first.
