# Users and sessions

## The login screen

When the computer starts the login screen appears: choose your user and type the password. At the
bottom are the computer's IP, the language, the keyboard and the restart and shut down buttons.

## Several sessions at once

- **Lock** (**❖L**): back to the login screen with your user; your apps stay open.
- **Switch user** (logo menu): someone else logs in with their account while your session keeps
  running in the background. A green dot marks the users with an open session.
- **Log out:** ends your apps and returns to the login screen.

## Accounts

In [Settings → Users](settings:users) an administrator adds and removes users, makes them
administrators and turns on **automatic login**. Everyone changes their own password (with the
current one) and avatar. New users already have their folders (Desktop, Documents, Downloads,
Music, Pictures, Videos) in their language.
