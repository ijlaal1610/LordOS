# Screenshots

Screenshots and screen recording.

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| ❖⇧3 · Print Screen | The whole screen |
| ❖⇧4 | An area (with Space, a window; Esc cancels) |
| ❖⇧5 | The panel: screen, window or area, timer, destination and record |

- The screenshot goes to `~/Pictures/Screenshots` or the clipboard, and a **thumbnail** shows in
  the corner: click it to open the **editor** and add arrows, boxes, text or blur.
- **Record the screen:** to MP4, with the microphone if you want. ❖⇧5 stops it.
- The first time, the system asks for **screen permission**.

Protected windows (for example password windows) come out black in screenshots, recordings and
remote desktop.
