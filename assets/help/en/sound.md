# Sound

- **Volume:** Control Center, or the volume keys.
- **Output and input:** [Settings → Sound](settings:sound) chooses speakers, headphones or HDMI,
  the microphone and its level (with a live meter).
- **Per app:** the volume of each app that's playing, and which output it goes to.
- **Test:** the test button plays the notification sound.

USB headphones appear by themselves when connected.
