# Fonts

To browse and manage the computer's fonts.

- **Collections:** all, yours, monospaced and color, with search by family.
- **Sample:** type some text and choose the size (8 to 120 px).
- **Characters:** the Unicode table of what the font has.
- **Info:** family, style, glyphs, file and size.
- **Install:** open a `.ttf`, `.otf` or `.ttc` from [Files](help:files) and press “Install”: it is
  installed just for your user, with no password.
- **Remove:** the ones you installed go to the Trash.
