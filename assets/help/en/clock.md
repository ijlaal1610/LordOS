# Clock

World clock, alarms, timer and stopwatch.

## World clock

**Add city** and search by name (“Tokyo”, “Madrid”) or by zone. Each city shows its time, whether
it is today, tomorrow or yesterday there, and the difference from your time. Hover a row and press
**×** to remove it. Times come from the system time zone database (tzdata), including daylight
saving changes.

## Alarms

- **New alarm:** the time, the days it repeats (or none, for just once) and a label.
- The switch turns it on or off; clicking an alarm edits or deletes it.
- When it rings a [notification](help:notifications) appears with sound: **Stop** or
  **Snooze 9 min**.
- **They ring even when Clock is closed**, as long as your session is open (also after switching
  users or with the session in the background). If the computer was suspended, an alarm missed by
  less than 10 minutes still rings when it wakes.
- The next alarm within 24 hours shows in the menu bar (⏰).

## Timer

Choose hours, minutes and seconds (or a shortcut: 1, 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 min) and **Start**. It can
be paused and resumed. It keeps running with the window closed and the time left shows in the menu
bar (⏱): clicking it brings Clock back.

## Stopwatch

Start, **Lap** and Stop. Laps show the split and the total; the fastest in green and the slowest
in red.

To change your time zone: [Settings → Language & Region](settings:language).
