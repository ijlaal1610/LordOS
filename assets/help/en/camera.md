# Camera

The webcam: live preview, JPEG photos and videos with sound.

- **Photo** or **video** with the big button; video also records the microphone.
- **Mirror:** flips the preview (like a mirror).
- If there are several cameras, choose which one to use.
- Photos and videos go to `~/Pictures` and `~/Videos`.

The first time the system asks for **camera** permission (and microphone to record video). A dot
shows in the menu bar while it is in use. Permissions are changed in
[Settings → Privacy](settings:privacy).
