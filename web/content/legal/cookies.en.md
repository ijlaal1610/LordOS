## No cookies

lordos.org sets **no cookies**: no analytics, advertising, tracking or session cookies, and none from third parties. That's why the website shows no cookie banner.

## What the website keeps in your browser

Two small preferences are saved in your browser's `localStorage`, on your device only:

| Key | Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `lordos-theme` | `light` or `dark` | remembers the theme you chose with the theme button; with automatic, nothing is saved and the site follows your system |
| `lordos-lang-seen` | `1` | shows the suggestion to read the site in your language only once |

They are never sent to a server, they don't identify you, and they are not shared with anyone. If your browser blocks storage, the website still works: it just forgets your choices.

## How to remove them

Delete the site data for lordos.org in your browser's settings (usually under Privacy → Site data or Cookies and site data). Private windows remove them when you close the window.

## Other sites

When you follow a link to GitHub, PayPal, Mercado Pago or any other site, that site's own cookie policy applies.
