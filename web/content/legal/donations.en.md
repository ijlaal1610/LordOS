LordOS is free and will stay free. Donations help the IPERFEX group keep building it: development time, testing on real hardware and hosting.

## A donation is voluntary

Donating is entirely optional. Everything on lordos.org and in LordOS is available whether you donate or not.

## Not a purchase

A donation is a gift, not a payment for goods or services. In exchange for it you receive no product, license, support, priority, features, influence over the roadmap, or any other benefit. We don't promise any specific result from the money we receive.

## Payment processors

Donations are processed entirely by third parties:

- **PayPal** (to `paypal@iperfex.com`), under [PayPal's terms](https://www.paypal.com/legalhub/home) and privacy statement;
- **Mercado Pago** (`link.mercadopago.com.ar/lordos`), under [Mercado Pago's terms](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/ayuda/terminos-y-politicas_194);
- **Binance**, when it becomes available, under Binance's terms.

We never see or store your card or bank details. The processor may charge fees, apply currency conversion, or refuse or reverse a payment under its own rules.
