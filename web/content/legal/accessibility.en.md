We want lordos.org to be usable by everyone, including people who use a keyboard only, a screen reader, magnification or high contrast. Our goal is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. This statement covers the website; the accessibility of the LordOS distribution is described at the end.

## What the website does today

- **Contrast:** text and controls use strong colour contrast in both the light and the dark theme, aiming at the WCAG AA ratios.
- **Light and dark:** the site follows your system's colour scheme, and the theme button lets you force light or dark.
- **Keyboard:** every link, button and control can be reached and used with the keyboard. A visible focus outline shows where you are, and a "Skip to content" link is the first thing on each page. The screenshot viewer works with the arrow keys and closes with Escape.
- **Reduced motion:** if your system asks for reduced motion, the site turns off its animations and smooth scrolling.
- **Structure:** pages use headings, landmarks and labelled sections; images have text alternatives; each page declares its language (English, Spanish or Portuguese).
- **Zoom and small screens:** the layout adapts to phone screens and to browser zoom.
- **No dependence on scripts:** the content is static HTML; scripts add only conveniences such as the theme button, the copy button and the gallery filters.

## Known limitations

- Some screenshots show complex interfaces; their text alternatives describe them briefly rather than in full detail.
- The paper's PDF may not be fully tagged for screen readers; the same content is available as a web page.
- Donation pages are run by PayPal and Mercado Pago, and their accessibility is outside our control.

## The LordOS distribution

Accessibility in the desktop is still in progress: LordOS has a **Reduce motion** setting (Settings → Appearance), keyboard shortcuts and per-display scaling from 100 to 200 %, but a screen reader, screen zoom and a high-contrast theme are **not available yet**. They are on the roadmap.

## Report a problem

If something on the website is hard or impossible to use, tell us through [our contact form](https://www.iperfex.com/contacto/?lang=en): the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser and assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within 5 business days and to fix problems in the next website update.
